# Room and laboratory recovery

Implemented in the split backend and wicars-ui repositories on 2026-10-23.

## User workflow

1. VPAA opens a building in Rooms and selects **Mark Unavailable** as the last action in an available physical room's table row or grid card. The existing confirmation modal identifies the room, collects the required closure reason, and confirms the action without a separate room selector. Unavailable rooms show **Mark Available** in the same position; reopening uses the existing room-update endpoint and confirmation modal without requiring a closure reason. The row updates immediately after success; the existing room live event refreshes Secretary Room Transfer. Unavailable rooms are excluded from new transfer suggestions and rechecked before transfers are saved. Edit Room manages details only: it has no status control or availability action, and detail saves omit status to preserve the room's current availability. New-room creation retains its status dropdown.
2. Closure records the original affected assignments per department and semester. Grant return/revocation records the same information before the existing Room-TBA release. Neither operation automatically relocates classes.
3. Secretary Dashboard and Room Requests display scoped **Room and Laboratory Alerts** only when unresolved recovery cases or pending room transfers need attention. Loading, an empty queue, and newly added rooms alone do not display the banner; failed checks retain a separate Retry message. Unresolved counts are computed from current assignments, so manual repairs and reopening are reflected without marking a notification read as a resolution. Repeated incidents deduplicate visible meetings.
4. Schedule Builder's toolbar uses **Room Transfer** in place of the Conflicts button. Its badge counts visible on-site meetings needing a room in the active semester, including Room TBA without a closure incident. It hides at zero. This count is not the borrowing-approval queue or the number of historical incidents. Schedule Management uses this toolbar button without a duplicate unavailable-room banner. Existing Dashboard alerts remain and their links open the transfer modal with the incident and semester selected. General conflict diagnostics remain reachable from individual timetable cards and do not automatically interrupt room transfer review.
5. The modal automatically uses the system's active semester and displays its academic year and semester name in the modal header without a selector or separate semester card. Verified incident links still resolve their own term automatically and label historical terms **Incident semester**. The schedule table stays on the left on desktop, with course, section, current room, day/time, required room type and transfer notice. Only meetings needing a room appear; there is no **Show all** checkbox. Up to 30 affected meetings are preselected; larger incidents are reviewed in batches.
6. Select meetings, then choose **Find suitable rooms** in the right-hand action row below the review card. The finder searches all eligible available rooms; there is no destination dropdown or search field. Results stay in the review card, and both finder and confirmation buttons sit outside it. The right pane stacks below the table on smaller screens. **Allow day and time changes** is off by default. When enabled, the search tries the original day/time first and includes alternatives across all seven days, automatically including Sunday even when the department's Sunday setting is off. Preview uses a separate search snapshot and does not change settings. VPAA approval of a selected Sunday transfer enables Sunday classes for that department in the same transaction, so the saved timetable remains valid in existing validators. Weekday-only selections do not change that setting. Linked groups are validated and saved atomically. The search first tries moving only the affected physical meeting while keeping its online partner in place; numeric custom day-pair markers are refreshed to describe the new placement. Named fixed-day patterns, consecutive-day and other group requirements remain hard rules. Whole-group shifts remain available when those rules require both meetings to move.
7. Review complete proposed groups and choose **Submit for approval**. The Secretary confirms in the existing modal: **Are you sure you want to submit this room transfer for approval?** The request becomes **Pending Dean Approval** and the department Dean is notified. Dean approval forwards it as **Pending VPAA Approval** and notifies the VPAA. Both roles review requests from **Room transfer requests** on their Schedule Approval pages. Existing schedules remain unchanged until final VPAA approval. Approval rechecks the selected subset against unselected schedules and saves atomically; rejection retains the original timetable and records the reason. See [Dean and VPAA room transfer approval](room-transfer-approval.md) for decision states, notifications, retry behavior and deployment. Results identify each course, section, day/time and current/replacement room; unchanged online partners are labeled explicitly. Unresolved results use the same readable labels instead of internal schedule IDs, and distinguish blocked transfers from a room search. The request status refreshes after submission; the timetable refreshes after approval. The modal supports Escape, focus trapping, and returning focus to its opener; background polling pauses during review.

Newly created available physical rooms appear as lower-priority opportunities for 14 days, filtered by current room visibility and access. Registration alone never moves classes. The finder considers existing and new rooms without a separate relocation engine. The API retains its optional destination-room filter for other callers.

## API

All routes require the existing authenticated/active-account middleware.

| Operation | Authorization |
|---|---|
| GET /api/room-recovery | schedule.view; current schedule/program read scope restricted to the reader's owning department |
| POST /api/rooms/{room}/close | schedule.view plus existing VPAA role restriction; reason required |
| POST /api/room-recovery/preview | schedule.update; explicit persisted ownership and program checks; at most 30 selected meetings |
| POST /api/room-recovery/plans/{plan}/submit (or /apply compatibility alias) | schedule.update; creator-owned stored plan; Secretary submits complete groups for Dean then VPAA approval |
| POST /api/room-recovery/plans/{plan}/approve or /reject | Department Dean plus schedule.approve_dean forwards to VPAA; VPAA plus schedule.approve_vpaa applies after Dean approval; either stage may reject with a reason |

GET accepts semester_id and include_schedules. It returns pending_count for the selected (default: active) semester even when schedules are omitted. The count and needs_attention flags share the same room assessment and read scope; duplicate incidents do not inflate the count. Historical incident links keep the toolbar count scoped to the active term. Alerts retain explicit semester identity. Preview plans require one department and semester, expire after 20 minutes, and store source identity/hash, scheduling snapshot fingerprint, before/after groups, and an applied selection. An identical submission or approval retry returns the existing outcome; a submitted selection is immutable and only final VPAA approval after Dean approval applies it. Submitted requests outlive the preview expiration.

Teaching delegation does not transfer responsibility for rooms. A Business Administration class taught by IT remains in Business Administration's room-transfer list and alerts. IT retains its existing schedule visibility for instructor assignment, but its room-transfer list, affected counts, and incident selections only include IT-owned schedules. Existing program and status visibility restrictions still apply.

## Validation and persistence

- Reuses SchedulingSnapshotRepository with all unchanged occupancy retained, AvailableSlotFinder, PlacementGroupValidator, BatchConflictValidator, and final RuleEngine validation.
- Uses bounded group candidate enumeration and constrained-first batch search. Partial recovery and search-limit results are explicit; no result is not proof of impossibility.
- Keeps course, instructor, ownership, duration, delivery, and group requirements. Laboratory and borrowing restrictions remain hard rules. No new capacity/equipment schema is implied.
- Preview does not reserve destinations or modify schedules. Only exact selected group rows are ignored/replaced during validation.
- Closure and room-request mutations acquire sorted semester scheduling locks before their transactions. Closure locks every existing semester, including terms without source-room bookings. Submission and approval lock the semester, plan, department, source/destination rooms and schedule rows before fresh validation; only approval writes schedules.
- All existing physical rooms must close through the reason-bearing closure endpoint, including vacant rooms and rooms booked outside the active term. The structural update service enforces this using the locked current room state. Direct status edits return validation guidance without saving other submitted changes. Room creation and reopening retain their existing paths. Expected Edit Room validation appears inside the form rather than opening a System Error dialog.
- Stored proposals are rejected if source assignments, relevant occupancy, room state, grants, or snapshot rules change. Approval rechecks the selected subset and validates final saved state before commit.
- Updates retain schedule IDs and use existing schedule history/audit tables. Unchanged linked partners are validated but not written, including their timestamps. If a numeric custom day pair changes, both partners receive the updated marker while the unaffected partner keeps its day, time and room. The two new tables are room_recovery_cases and room_recovery_plans.
- Room Transfer supports `faculty_assignment` as well as the existing plotting-editable statuses. With **Allow day and time changes** enabled, these meetings and their complete linked groups can move to Sunday or another valid day/time. The existing instructor, assignment completion and status are preserved. Instructor activity, availability and overlapping bookings remain hard constraints in preview and save. Without the option, the original day and time are checked again under the approval lock. Other protected stages still require the normal revision workflow. General plotting/edit endpoints keep their existing safeguards; no status is automatically reset.
- Closure notifications run after commit. Transfer submission/decision notifications persist atomically with their request or decision; realtime delivery is flushed after the request and cache exceptions are logged. Existing live topics plus Dashboard polling/focus refresh update alerts.

## Loading and badge updates

- List loading eagerly fetches schedule references and checks only room availability, type and access using the existing room rules. Per-batch lookup caches share grant, lending and program-sharing facts across meetings. It does not run curriculum, instructor, duration or overlap validation just to display the list. Preview, submission and approval retain their full validation.
- Incident queries first restrict the owning department and assess each visible meeting once per batch. No room-assessment results are cached across API requests, so grant approval/rejection, room reopening and manual repairs are reflected on the next read.
- The panel preloads the list and badge together. Opening within 10 seconds reuses that component's loaded response; older data is fetched again. Room, schedule, approval, course, section, department and settings events invalidate this reuse. Focus and 60-second polling refresh while closed, and successful transfers refresh immediately. No badge request duplicates the panel request.
- A local read-only controller benchmark on 2026-10-23 used the same secretary scope, 151 on-site meetings and 17 pending meetings. The full-list read fell from 2,130 SQL queries / 1,929.5 ms to 25 queries / 53.4–56.5 ms. These timings exclude HTTP transport, browser rendering and deployment load. The summary read fell from 220 queries / 333.4 ms to 25 queries / 155.1 ms on its first measured invocation; it now also returns the pending count.

## Installation

Start the application's MySQL service, then run from C:/Users/toman/backend:

```powershell
php artisan migrate --path=database/migrations/2026_10_23_120000_create_room_recovery_tables.php
```

Also run the additive migration in [Dean and VPAA room transfer approval](room-transfer-approval.md), then deploy the matching frontend build. The feature is exposed directly in the authenticated UI; there is no new feature flag. The initial integration could not apply the migration because MySQL was stopped and Windows denied permission to start it. During the subsequent workflow review, MySQL was available and migrate:status confirmed the recovery migration had already run (batch 20). No additional migration was needed during that review.

## Verification and limitations

Focused tests cover closure idempotency, scoped alerts, protected approvals, laboratory rejection, same-time and opt-in time recovery, linked groups, competing destinations, history failure rollback, stale room state, repeated submission and approval, grant-loss capture, and read-only new-room preview. Additional regressions cover same-time room replacements during faculty assignment, original-row identity and unrelated-row preservation, unchanged online partners, blocked timing changes, and bookings created after Preview. Frontend checks cover exact deep links, selected classes, settings invalidating previews, stale submission and approval errors, failed alert loads, readable unresolved results and matching before/after rows by ID.

The seven-class Sunday failure was reproduced against the current MySQL data before changes. The first hybrid group consumed all 1,600 candidate validations, leaving none for the other six groups. Its numeric custom day marker also rejected changed days, and the search unnecessarily shifted its already-valid online partner. Sunday was present in the weekday mapping and search snapshot; this was candidate generation and validation starvation, not a Sunday query/date conversion error.

Candidate generation now shares the existing budget between groups, filters incompatible room types before full validation, reuses each group's validation context, and spreads retained slot alternatives across available days. It reserves three seconds of the existing 12-second limit for batch planning and stops once all candidate-bearing groups have valid placements. Only numeric custom day markers are refreshed; named fixed patterns and instructor conflict/availability rules remain enforced. A rolled-back preview against the same live data found valid Sunday candidates for all seven groups and a complete seven-transfer plan in about four seconds, including one Sunday transfer. No real schedules were moved. The UI only suggests trying fewer classes when the bounded search leaves unresolved groups.

Verification passed 106 focused backend tests (1,857 assertions), 34 frontend tests, PHP formatting, targeted frontend lint and the production build. New regressions list and apply replacements on every weekday, save Sunday transfers with an assigned instructor and a linked online partner, and apply seven competing hybrid groups on Sunday without moving their online partners or introducing conflicts.

MySQL multi-connection contention and a live browser/database walkthrough remain unverified. Date-range closures, moving unrelated classes to make space, changing instructor/delivery mode, and optimality guarantees remain outside this implementation. Search budgets are 12 seconds, 1,600 candidate validations, and 2,000 batch nodes; the underlying slot finder also has its existing bound. Large incidents can be handled in reviewed batches of up to 30 selected meetings.
